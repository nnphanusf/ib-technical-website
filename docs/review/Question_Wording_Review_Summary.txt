INTERPRETATION
This is an editorial review, not a renewed verification of financial answers. Automated screens supported a full reading of the active inventory. Flags are review candidates, not declarations that the question is wrong. Missing-input flags distinguish incomplete practice cases from open-ended concepts where possible.
Direct questions take question marks; instructions such as “Walk me through...” may correctly end with periods. Acronyms and proper names retain capitals.
Some malformed source fragments remain flagged rather than being rewritten into invented questions.
